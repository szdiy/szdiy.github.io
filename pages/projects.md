* [The Ultimate Hacking Matrix](https://groups.google.com/g/szdiy/c/Dh-xHqzdOTE): DIY keyboard.
* [TODO](https://groups.google.com/g/szdiy/c/VhhY53n2LFQ): Terminal Style TODO Project.
